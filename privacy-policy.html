Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 20 June 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to PlainTalk Tech (“we”, “us”, “our”). We provide AI-automation audits and related services to small businesses. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you visit https://plaintalktech.ie or complete our lead forms.

2. Data We Collect

  • Contact Information: Full name, work email address, phone number (optional).
  • Business Details: Short answer responses (e.g. “What’s your biggest admin headache?”).
  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, cookies and similar technologies.

3. How We Collect Data

Lead Forms: When you complete our Facebook Lead Ad or website contact forms.

Website Cookies: We use essential cookies for site functionality and analytics cookies (e.g. Google Analytics) to improve our site.

4. Purpose & Legal Basis

We process your data to:

  • Provide the free 30-min workflow audit and follow-up scheduling.
  • Send you emails/SMS reminders about your audit.
  • Improve our services and website.

Our legal bases are your consent (form submission) and our legitimate interest (service delivery).

5. Sharing & Disclosure

We will never sell your personal data. We may share it with:

  • Service Providers: e.g., Calendly, email/SMS platforms, analytics tools—only to the extent needed to deliver our audit.
  • Legal Authorities: if required by law.

6. Cookies & Tracking

Essential Cookies: keep you logged in and maintain your session.

Performance Cookies: Google Analytics tracks anonymous site usage.

You can manage or disable cookies via your browser settings, but some features may then not work.

7. Data Retention

Leads & Contacts: retained for up to 2 years unless you request earlier deletion.

Analytics Data: aggregated site data retained for 14 months.

8. Your Rights (GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Access your data.
  • Rectify inaccuracies.
  • Erase your data (“right to be forgotten”).
  • Restrict or object to processing.
  • Request data portability.

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@plaintalktech.ie.

9. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.
